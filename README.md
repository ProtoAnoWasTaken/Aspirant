## ASPIRANT. THE CLOCKWORK BEHIND THE CARDS
**Aspirant** is a mod for Balatro inspired by the esoteric puzzle adventure games by the [Dragonfish](https://www.roblox.com/communities/3945802/Dragonfish) development team, spearheaded by Termiteking9, otherwise known collectively as [*Termiteking9's Gears*](https://www.roblox.com/games/3372306786/Gears). Aspirant is a love letter to the community around *Gears*, featuring both canon game content and (planned to involve) community content, such as fangames and in-jokes.

This mod was fully scripted by [ProtoAno](https://github.com/ProtoAnoWasTaken) (ProtoAnoWasTaken), and basically fully illustrated (AND MODELED) by [Faow](https://github.com/faowfanatic-cell) (Faowbot). Conceptualization for mod content largely comes from both, with the occasional community submission.
